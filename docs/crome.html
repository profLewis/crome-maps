Crop Map of England (CROME)

Summary

Annual field-level crop type map of England produced by the Rural Payments Agency (RPA), classifying ~32 million hexagonal cells into 15+ crop types at 20m resolution. Derived from Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical imagery using random forest classification trained on LPIS/IACS declarations. Available 2016-2024.

Description

The Crop Map of England (CROME) is published annually by the Rural Payments Agency as part of Defra's open data initiative. It uses a random forest classifier trained on farmer-declared crop types from the IACS/BPS system, applied to Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-2 optical time series. The map covers all agricultural land in England using a hexagonal grid (~20m cell size). Major classes include wheat, barley, oilseed rape, maize, potatoes, sugar beet, beans, peas, and various grassland types.

Provenance

Produced by RPA as part of the Control with Remote Sensing (CwRS) programme for the Basic Payment Scheme (BPS). Training data from ~2.6 million declared agricultural parcels. Classification: random forest on Sentinel-1 VV/VH backscatter + Sentinel-2 spectral bands + temporal features. Post-processing: majority vote per hexagonal cell, smoothing, and land parcel boundary alignment.

Classification System

15+ crop types: Winter Wheat, Spring Wheat, Winter Barley, Spring Barley, Oilseed Rape, Maize, Potatoes, Sugar Beet, Field Beans, Peas, Linseed, Grass (< 5yr), Grass (> 5yr), Non-agricultural Land, Fallow, plus minor classes.

Accuracy & Validation

RPA conducts annual validation using Ground Observation Points (GOP) — over 11,000 field-visited locations per year. Overall accuracy varies by year: v2017 86% (kappa 0.85), v2020 70.5% (kappa 0.66), v2023 90.12% (OOB error 4.98%). The v2023 improvement reflects the switch to Planet Fusion imagery alongside Sentinel-1.
