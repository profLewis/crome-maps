New Zealand Land Cover Database (LCDB)

Summary

National land cover classification for New Zealand at 1:50,000 scale, produced by Landcare Research/Manaaki Whenua. The LCDB maps 33 land cover classes across all of New Zealand including cropland, horticulture, and pastoral classes. Updated approximately every 5 years from 1996.

CoverageNew Zealand
Resolution1:50,000 (polygon, ~1ha MMU)
Temporal1996, 2001, 2008, 2012, 2018
LicenseCC-BY 4.0

Description

The LCDB provides a multi-temporal, thematic classification of land cover for mainland New Zealand, the Chatham Islands, and some offshore islands. Agricultural classes include short-rotation cropland, orchard/vineyard/nursery, high producing grassland, low producing grassland, and exotic forest.

Provenance

Landcare Research / Manaaki Whenua. Semi-automated classification from Landsat and SPOT imagery, with extensive manual editing and quality control.

Classification System

33 classes including: Short-rotation Cropland, Orchard/Vineyard/Nursery, High Producing Grassland, Low Producing Grassland

Overall accuracy ~94% at level 1. Cropland classes achieve >85% accuracy.
