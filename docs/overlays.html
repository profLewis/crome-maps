Environmental Overlays

Summary

Contextual environmental datasets from JRC and other sources that complement crop and land cover data. Includes Land Productivity Dynamics (vegetation productivity trends), River Flood Hazard (100-year return period flood extent), and INCA ecosystem service indicators (crop provision capacity, crop pollination potential). These overlays provide environmental context for agricultural analysis.

CoverageEurope (varies by dataset)
ResolutionVaries (300m-1km)
TemporalVaries (2015-2023)
LicenseVarious (JRC open data, Copernicus)

Description

The overlay datasets provide environmental context that is relevant to crop production but is not itself a land cover classification. These datasets help answer questions like: Is vegetation productivity increasing or declining? What areas face flood risk? Where are ecosystem services like pollination strongest? - Land Productivity Dynamics (LPD): Trends in vegetation productivity from NDVI time series. Classifies areas as increasing, stable, or declining. - River Flood Hazard: 100-year return period flood extent from hydrological modeling. - INCA Crop Provision: Ecosystem capacity to provide crops, from the MAES/INCA framework. - INCA Crop Pollination: Pollination service potential for agriculture.

Provenance

LPD: JRC, derived from SPOT-VGT/PROBA-V NDVI time series. Flood Hazard: JRC LISFLOOD model under EFAS (European Flood Awareness System). INCA: JRC MAES (Mapping and Assessment of Ecosystem Services) initiative.

Classification System

Various continuous/categorical indicators (see individual layer descriptions)

Accuracy & Validation

LPD: Based on well-established NDVI trend analysis; validated against ground observations and national statistics. Flood Hazard: Calibrated against historical flood events. INCA: Modeled indicators, validated against field measurements where available.
