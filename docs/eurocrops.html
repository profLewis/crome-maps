EuroCrops V2

Summary

Harmonized EU-wide crop parcel dataset covering 18 countries from 2008-2023, with crop types mapped to a unified HCAT v4 taxonomy. Contains millions of individual field parcels derived from national LPIS/IACS declarations. V2 significantly expands V1 coverage (16 to 18 countries, 2008-2023 vs 2018-2021).

Description

EuroCrops V2 combines self-declared crop reporting datasets from EU member states into a harmonized pan-European dataset. Each parcel includes the original national crop code, translated to the Hierarchical Crop and Agriculture Taxonomy (HCAT v4). Countries included: AT, BE (Flanders/Wallonia), BG, CZ, DE (Brandenburg/NRW), DK, EE, ES, FI, FR, IE, IT (Tuscany), NL, PT, SI, SK. V2 adds Croatia and Latvia.

Provenance

Created by the Joint Research Centre (JRC) of the European Commission, building on the original EuroCrops project by TU Munich. Source data from national LPIS/IACS databases. HCAT v4 taxonomy provides cross-country harmonization of crop types.

Classification System

HCAT v4 (Hierarchical Crop and Agriculture Taxonomy) — 4 levels with ~200 crop types

Accuracy & Validation

Based on official LPIS/IACS declarations — self-reported by farmers and verified by national agencies through CwRS. Crop type accuracy is generally high (>95%) for major crops, as declarations are tied to EU subsidy payments. Geometric accuracy depends on the national LPIS system (typically <2m for GNSS-based).

EuroCrops is built from administrative declarations, not remote sensing classification. The crop type labels reflect what farmers declared and agencies verified. LUCAS 2022 ground truth can be used for independent cross-validation.
