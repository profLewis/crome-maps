China Land Cover Dataset (CLCD)

Summary

Annual 30m land cover classification for all of China from 1985 to present. Uses Landsat imagery to map 9 land cover types including cropland. The longest high-resolution land cover time series for China, enabling analysis of 30+ years of agricultural land use change.

CoverageChina
Resolution30m (Landsat)

Description

CLCD provides annual 30m land cover maps for China using all available Landsat imagery (5/7/8). Nine classes include cropland, forest, shrub, grassland, water, snow/ice, barren, impervious, and wetland. The long time series enables tracking of cropland expansion, urbanization, and reforestation in China.

Provenance

Produced by Wuhan University using Google Earth Engine. Classification: random forest on Landsat spectral-temporal features. Training data from field surveys and very high resolution imagery.

Classification System

9 classes: Cropland, Forest, Shrub, Grassland, Water, Snow/Ice, Barren, Impervious (urban), Wetland.

Accuracy & Validation

Overall accuracy 79.3% (2020). Cropland: 85.7% producer accuracy, 83.2% user accuracy. Validated against independent test samples and comparison with ESA WorldCover.
