SAGE Global Crop Calendars

Summary

Global gridded planting and harvest dates at 0.5° resolution for 19 major crops. Compiled by the Center for Sustainability and the Global Environment (SAGE) at the University of Wisconsin-Madison from national and subnational agricultural calendars. Provides median planting and harvest day-of-year for each grid cell. The standard reference for global crop phenology.

Description

The SAGE crop calendars provide spatially explicit planting and harvest dates for 19 crops globally. For each crop and grid cell, the dataset gives the median planting date and harvest date as day-of-year (1-365). Data was compiled from national agricultural ministry reports, FAO country briefs, USDA crop calendars, and regional agronomic literature. The dataset represents "typical" or "average" planting/harvest timing, not year-specific dates.

Provenance

Compiled by Sacks et al. at SAGE, University of Wisconsin-Madison. Sources include USDA FAS Crop Calendars, FAO GIEWS Country Briefs, national agricultural extension services, and published agronomic literature. Spatial interpolation fills gaps between source observations.

Classification System

19 crops: Wheat, Winter Wheat, Maize, Rice, Soybeans, Barley, Cotton, Sorghum, Potatoes, Winter Rapeseed, plus others. Each crop has planting date and harvest date maps shown as day-of-year (1 = January 1, 365 = December 31).

Accuracy & Validation

Planting/harvest dates are compiled from authoritative sources and represent typical timing. Accuracy varies by region: well-constrained in countries with detailed agronomic data (US, Europe, Australia), less certain in tropical developing countries with multiple growing seasons. Typical uncertainty is ±2-4 weeks in well-documented regions, potentially ±4-8 weeks in data-sparse areas.
