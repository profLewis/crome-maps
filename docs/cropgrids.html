CROPGRIDS — Global Gridded Crop Areas

Summary

Global gridded dataset of crop harvested areas at ~5km resolution (2.5 arc-min) for 173 crops. CROPGRIDSv1.08 provides the most comprehensive global view of where each crop is grown, combining national statistics with satellite-derived cropland maps. Covers 15 key crops in the viewer: wheat, maize, rice, soybean, barley, potato, sugarcane, cotton, rapeseed, sunflower, cassava, sorghum, millet, groundnut, and coffee.

Description

CROPGRIDS disaggregates national and subnational crop production statistics onto a global grid using satellite-derived cropland extent maps as spatial weights. For each grid cell, it provides the harvested area (in hectares) for 173 individual crops. The dataset harmonizes data from FAO, national statistical agencies, and satellite observations.

Provenance

Produced by IIASA (International Institute for Applied Systems Analysis). Combines FAO national statistics, subnational statistics from SPAM, and satellite-derived cropland extent from MODIS/ESA CCI. Spatial disaggregation uses an entropy-based cross-entropy optimization approach.

Classification System

173 individual crops grouped into categories: cereals (wheat, maize, rice, barley, sorghum, millet, oats, rye), oilseeds (soybean, rapeseed, sunflower, groundnut), roots/tubers (potato, cassava, sweet potato), fiber (cotton), stimulants (coffee, tea, cocoa), sugar (sugarcane, sugar beet), and more.

Accuracy & Validation

National-level crop areas match FAOSTAT closely: R²=0.97 for national crop-specific area, R²=0.99 for global totals (48 crops within ±10%). Major crops (wheat, maize, rice, soybean, barley, rapeseed, cotton, cassava, sunflower, sugarcane, oil palm) have R²>0.95 and NRMSE≤0.05 vs national/subnational statistics. Total world harvested area: ~1.5 billion hectares covering 173 crops.

CROPGRIDS should be interpreted as a statistical disaggregation, not a direct observation. Individual grid cells may not perfectly reflect local conditions, but regional aggregates are constrained to official statistics.
