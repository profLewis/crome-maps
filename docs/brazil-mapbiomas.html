Brazil MapBiomas

Summary

Annual 30m land cover and land use maps of Brazil from 1985 to present, produced by the MapBiomas collaborative network. Covers all Brazilian biomes with detailed agricultural classes including soybeans, sugarcane, coffee, cotton, rice, and pasture. The reference dataset for monitoring deforestation and agricultural expansion in Brazil.

Description

MapBiomas uses the Google Earth Engine platform to process all available Landsat imagery for Brazil, producing annual land cover maps with 30+ classes. Agricultural classes include temporary crops (soybean, maize, cotton, rice), semi-perennial crops (sugarcane), perennial crops (coffee, citrus, oil palm), and pasture. The project also monitors deforestation alerts and water surface changes.

Provenance

Collaborative network including universities, NGOs, and technology companies (SEEG/OC, IPAM, IMAZON, ISA, others). Uses random forest classification in Google Earth Engine with extensive training data from field campaigns and high-resolution imagery.

Classification System

30+ classes including: Soy Beans, Sugar Cane, Rice, Cotton, Coffee, Citrus, Other Temporary Crops, Other Perennial Crops, Pasture, Forest, Savanna, Mangrove, Wetland, Urban, Mining, Water, plus biome-specific classes.

Accuracy & Validation

Overall accuracy 89.8% (Collection 8, 2021). Agricultural classes perform well: Soy 92%, Sugarcane 90%, Pasture 87%. Forest/non-forest distinction >95%.
