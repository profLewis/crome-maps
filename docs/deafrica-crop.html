Digital Earth Africa — Cropland Extent

Summary

Annual cropland extent map for the African continent at 10m resolution from Digital Earth Africa. Uses Sentinel-2 imagery to map where crops are grown, supporting food security monitoring across sub-Saharan Africa.

CoverageAfrica
Resolution10m (Sentinel-2)
Temporal2019-present (annual)
LicenseCC-BY 4.0

Description

Digital Earth Africa's cropland extent map provides annual continental-scale mapping of agricultural areas using Sentinel-2 time series. The product focuses on binary cropland/non-cropland classification with emphasis on capturing smallholder farming systems prevalent in sub-Saharan Africa.

Provenance

Produced by Digital Earth Africa (DEA), an initiative of the Group on Earth Observations (GEO). Uses the Open Data Cube platform for processing Sentinel-2 Analysis Ready Data.
