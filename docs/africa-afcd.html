Africa Cropland 30m (AFCD)

Summary

Continental-scale 30m cropland map of Africa derived from Sentinel-2 imagery. The African Field Crop Dataset provides binary cropland/non-cropland classification across the entire African continent, capturing both large-scale and smallholder agricultural systems. Critical for food security monitoring in sub-Saharan Africa.

Description

The AFCD maps cropland extent across Africa using Sentinel-2 optical imagery. The classification focuses on distinguishing cultivated areas from natural vegetation, a challenging task in Africa due to the prevalence of smallholder farming, intercropping, and agroforestry systems.

Provenance

Produced using machine learning applied to Sentinel-2 time series. Training data from field campaigns, very high resolution imagery, and national agricultural surveys.

Classification System

Binary: Cropland / Non-cropland

Accuracy & Validation

Overall accuracy ~85% for cropland/non-cropland. Higher accuracy in East Africa (Kenya, Ethiopia) where training data is more abundant. Lower accuracy in semi-arid transition zones (Sahel) and complex agroforestry systems (West Africa).
