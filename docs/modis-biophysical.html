MODIS LAI, FPAR & GPP

Summary

Global biophysical parameters from MODIS: Leaf Area Index (LAI), Fraction of Photosynthetically Active Radiation (FPAR), and Gross Primary Productivity (GPP). These complement vegetation indices by quantifying canopy structure, light interception, and carbon fixation rates at 500m–1km resolution.

CoverageGlobal
Resolution500m (LAI/FPAR), 500m (GPP)
Temporal2000–present (8-day composites)
LicensePublic domain (US Government work)

Description

LAI (Leaf Area Index) quantifies the one-sided green leaf area per unit ground surface area — a key structural parameter for crop growth models. FPAR measures the fraction of incoming solar radiation (400–700nm) absorbed by vegetation canopies for photosynthesis. GPP (Gross Primary Productivity) estimates the total amount of carbon fixed by vegetation through photosynthesis, critical for carbon cycle monitoring and crop yield estimation.

Provenance

NASA EOSDIS products MCD15A2H (LAI/FPAR) and MOD17A2H (GPP). LAI/FPAR: MODIS Combined Terra+Aqua 8-day composite. GPP: MODIS Terra 8-day composite using the Biome Properties Look-Up Table approach.

Classification System

Continuous values. LAI: 0–10 m²/m² (cropland typically 1–6). FPAR: 0–1.0 (fraction). GPP: 0–3000 g C/m²/yr.

Accuracy & Validation

LAI/FPAR validated to Stage 3 maturity. Uncertainty: LAI ±0.66 m²/m², FPAR ±0.12 for broadleaf crops. GPP validated against FLUXNET eddy covariance towers.
