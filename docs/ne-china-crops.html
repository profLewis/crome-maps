Northeast China Crop Types (30m)

Summary

30m crop type map of Northeast China (Heilongjiang, Jilin, Liaoning) — China's most important grain production region. Classifies maize, rice, soybeans, and other crops using Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery.

Description

Detailed crop type classification for China's "breadbasket" region, which produces ~30% of China's grain. Uses multi-temporal satellite imagery to distinguish major crops including maize, rice (paddy), soybeans, and wheat.

Provenance

Produced by Chinese Academy of Sciences using Sentinel-2 and Landsat-8 data with random forest classification. Training data from field surveys and government agricultural statistics.

Classification System

Major crops: Maize, Rice (paddy), Soybeans, Wheat, Other crops

Overall accuracy ~88%. Maize and rice achieve >90% accuracy due to distinctive phenological patterns. Soybean accuracy ~82%.
