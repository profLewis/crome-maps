CORINE Land Cover

Summary

Pan-European land cover/land use inventory at 100m resolution, covering 39 EEA countries with a 44-class nomenclature. Produced every 6 years since 1990 by the Copernicus Land Monitoring Service. The reference dataset for European land cover analysis with 30+ years of harmonized time series.

CoverageEurope (EEA-39)
Resolution100m (25ha minimum mapping unit)
Temporal1990, 2000, 2006, 2012, 2018 (6-year cycle)
LicenseCopernicus Data Policy (free, open)

Description

CORINE (Coordination of Information on the Environment) Land Cover provides consistent land cover information across Europe using a 44-class hierarchical nomenclature at three levels. Agricultural classes include arable land, permanent crops, pastures, and heterogeneous agricultural areas. Minimum mapping unit is 25 hectares (status layers) or 5 hectares (change layers). Produced by computer-aided visual interpretation of satellite imagery verified with ancillary data.

Provenance

Coordinated by the European Environment Agency (EEA) under the Copernicus Land Monitoring Service. National teams produce country-level data following common technical specifications. Input: Sentinel-2 (2018), Landsat/SPOT (earlier years). Quality assurance: independent verification per country.

Classification System

44 classes in 3 levels. Agricultural classes: 211 Non-irrigated arable, 212 Permanently irrigated, 213 Rice fields, 221-223 Vineyards/Fruit/Olive, 231 Pastures, 241-244 Heterogeneous agricultural areas.

Accuracy & Validation

Target thematic accuracy >= 85% at level 3 (44 classes). Achieved accuracy varies by country. The 2018 CLC achieved 87.0% overall accuracy based on LUCAS validation. Agricultural classes typically perform well (>85%) due to clear spectral signatures.
